🌐 About the Project

Hengdang ScamShield is a cybersecurity-focused web application developed to help individuals recognise potential online scams, phishing attempts and suspicious digital activities.

With the increasing number of digital transactions and online communications, fraudulent messages and malicious links have become serious threats. ScamShield aims to make digital safety more accessible by providing users with tools to examine suspicious content and understand potential security risks.

Developed under the Hengdang cybersecurity initiative, this project represents our effort to turn cybersecurity awareness into a practical and accessible digital solution.

🚀 Key Features

Feature| Description
💬 Message Scanner| Examine suspicious SMS, WhatsApp messages and other text-based communications.
🔗 URL Scanner| Analyse suspicious links and identify potential phishing indicators.
📷 QR Code Scanner| Scan or upload QR codes to extract and examine their embedded destinations.
⚠️ Threat Assessment| Present potential risk indicators and security warnings.
🧠 Scam Awareness| Help users recognise common online fraud techniques and suspicious behaviour.

🎯 What Scams Does ScamShield Aim to Address?

ScamShield focuses on common digital fraud scenarios, including:

- Phishing Attacks: Fake websites and deceptive links designed to steal personal information.
- Banking & KYC Scams: Fraudulent bank alerts asking users to update or verify their accounts.
- UPI & Payment Fraud: Fake payment requests, QR-code payment tricks and transaction deception.
- Courier Scams: Fake delivery notifications and parcel-related payment links.
- Job & Recruitment Scams: Fraudulent job offers demanding registration fees or personal information.
- Lottery & Reward Scams: Fake prizes and reward notifications used to manipulate victims.
- Government Impersonation: Messages pretending to represent official government departments.
- OTP Theft: Social engineering attempts designed to obtain one-time passwords.

🔍 How It Works

1. Submit: Enter a suspicious message, URL or QR code.
2. Examine: Use the relevant scanner to inspect the submitted content.
3. Identify: Review suspicious indicators and potential warning signs.
4. Understand: Learn why the content may be unsafe.
5. Stay Protected: Follow the recommended precautions before interacting with suspicious content.

</p>🔐 Security & Responsible Use

ScamShield is intended to support cybersecurity awareness and preliminary threat assessment. Its findings should not be treated as a guarantee that a message, URL or QR code is safe or malicious.

- Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or banking credentials.
- Avoid opening suspicious links.
- Do not scan or interact with unknown QR destinations without checking them.
- Verify sensitive requests through official channels.
- Report financial cyber fraud in India through 1930 or "cybercrime.gov.in" (https://cybercrime.gov.in/).
